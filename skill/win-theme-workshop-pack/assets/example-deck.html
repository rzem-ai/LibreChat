Final check · apply once each theme is drafted

Stress-test every win theme before it leaves the room

Run each draft statement through these seven checks. Sharpen anything a competitor could claim just as easily.

Why us
The client should choose us because…

Best fit · unique value · what the client gains by selecting us.

What makes us different
Unlike other bidders, we…

Our people · experience · delivery approach · tools or accelerators · relationships · way of working.

What worries the client
{{CLIENT_NAME}}'s biggest risk is… and we address it by…

Delivery risk · stakeholder engagement · timeframes · change management · technical complexity · governance cost.

Strongest evidence
Our strongest proof point is…

Similar projects · measurable outcomes · client testimonials · industry recognition · certifications and case studies · experienced personnel.

Client outcome
By choosing us, {{CLIENT_NAME}} will be able to…

Focus on outcomes, not activities: deliver sooner and reduce risk, improve services and build internal capability, achieve long-term sustainability.

Finish the sentence
We don't just provide ___, we provide ___.

technology → confidence · advice → practical outcomes · project management → successful delivery · resources → trusted partners.

Competitive gap
If we lose, what will the winning bidder have convinced {{CLIENT_NAME}} of?

Use the answer to close our own gaps and sharpen the message.

Believability test
If a competitor used this exact statement, would it still be believable?

Yes — it's too generic, sharpen it. No — that's a real win theme.
