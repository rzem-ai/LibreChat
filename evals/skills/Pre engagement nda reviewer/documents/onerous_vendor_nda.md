# MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

This Non-Disclosure Agreement (**Agreement**) is made between **Meridian Global Holdings Inc.**, a company incorporated in the State of Delaware, United States (**Discloser**), and the counterparty identified on the signature page (**Recipient**).

## 1. Purpose

1.1 The purpose of this Agreement is to enable the Discloser to share commercially sensitive information with the Recipient so that the Recipient may evaluate and deliver advisory services in connection with the Discloser's proposed acquisition of a regional logistics business (the **Purpose**).

## 2. Term

2.1 This Agreement commences on the date of last signature and continues in force for a period of five (5) years. The confidentiality obligations in clause 3 survive for a further five (5) years after expiry.

## 3. Confidentiality and Disclosure

3.1 The Recipient must keep all Confidential Information strictly confidential and must not disclose any Confidential Information to any person, including its own employees, officers, contractors or advisers, without the Discloser's prior written consent on each occasion.

3.2 The Recipient must not copy, reproduce or summarise any Confidential Information except as expressly approved in writing by the Discloser.

## 4. Data Handling and Technology

4.1 The Recipient must store, host and process all Confidential Information exclusively on servers physically located within the United States, and must not transfer, host or store any Confidential Information outside the United States for any reason.

4.2 The Recipient must not use any generative artificial intelligence, machine learning, or large language model tools of any kind to process, analyse, summarise or otherwise handle the Confidential Information.

## 5. Exclusivity

5.1 During the Term, the Recipient must not provide advisory, consulting or evaluation services to any other person in respect of the logistics or freight-forwarding sector in North America without the Discloser's prior written consent.

## 6. Non-Solicitation

6.1 During the Term and for twenty-four (24) months afterwards, neither Party may solicit, hire or engage any employee or contractor of the other Party who was involved in the Purpose.

## 7. Return and Destruction of Confidential Information

7.1 On termination or expiry of this Agreement, or on the Discloser's written request, the Recipient must immediately return or destroy all Confidential Information in its possession or control, and certify in writing that it has done so. The Recipient may not retain any copy of the Confidential Information for any purpose.

## 8. Conflicts of Interest

8.1 The Recipient must disclose in writing any actual, potential or perceived conflict of interest, and must not act for any party whose interests conflict with those of the Discloser during the Term.

## 9. Indemnity

9.1 The Recipient indemnifies and holds harmless the Discloser against any and all losses, damages, costs and expenses (including legal costs on a full indemnity basis) suffered or incurred by the Discloser arising directly or indirectly from any breach of this Agreement by the Recipient or its personnel.

## 10. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

10.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, United States. The Parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of the State of Delaware.

## 11. Miscellaneous

11.1 This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties and may only be amended in writing signed by both Parties. No warranty, express or implied, is given by either Party except as expressly set out in this Agreement.
