1. **CONFIDENTIALITY**

* 1. The Receiving Party shall keep the Disclosing Party’s Confidential Information strictly confidential for the duration of the Term.
  2. The Receiving Party shall implement measures for the duration of the Term to protect and safeguard the Disclosing Party’s Confidential Information from unauthorised disclosure, loss, theft, destruction, and/or use and to exercise in relation to the Confidential Information no lesser a standard of care than that which the Receiving Party applies to its own confidential information.
  3. During the Term, the Receiving Party may only use the Disclosing Party’s Confidential Information solely for and/or in connection with the Purpose and/or considering, discussing, evaluating and engaging in any project or transaction contemplated as part of the Purpose.
  4. The Receiving Party acknowledges and agrees that for the duration of the Term it shall:
     1. not disclose the Disclosing Party’s Confidential Information to any person, other than (a) a Permitted Recipient on a strict ‘need to know’ basis for the Purpose or (b) with the prior written consent of the Disclosing Party; and
     2. procure that Permitted Recipients to whom Confidential Information is disclosed are aware of, and fully comply with, the obligations of the Receiving Party under this Agreement as if the Permitted Recipient were a party to this Agreement. For the avoidance of doubt, the Receiving Party shall be liable for failure of any such Permitted Recipient to comply with the provisions of this Agreement, as though each such Permitted Recipient were a party to and had acted in breach of this Agreement.
  5. This Clause 2 shall not apply to Confidential Information which:
     1. is or becomes available to the public other than as a result of a disclosure in violation of this Agreement;
     2. was available to the Receiving Party prior to its disclosure to the Receiving Party and its Personnel by the Disclosing Party, through means of access or disclosure otherwise than in breach of any obligation of confidentiality to the Disclosing Party;
     3. was or is developed by the Receiving Party independently of, and without reference to, the Confidential Information;
     4. is the subject of a Mandatory Disclosure;
     5. the Disclosing Party agrees in writing may be disclosed; or
     6. is disclosed to the Receiving Party by a third party which, to the Receiving Party’s knowledge, is not being disclosed in breach of any obligation of confidentiality to the Disclosing Party.
  6. For the purpose of Clause 2.5.4, where the Receiving Party is required to make a Mandatory Disclosure during the Term, then the Receiving Party shall give the Disclosing Party as much notice of such disclosure as is reasonably practicable (provided Applicable Law permits the same).

1. **RETURN AND DESTRUCTION OF CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION**

* 1. Subject to Clauses 5.2 and 5.3, upon request by the Disclosing Party in writing at any time, the Receiving Party shall, and shall procure that its Permitted Recipients to whom Confidential Information is disclosed shall:
     1. immediately cease all evaluation and use of the Confidential Information;
     2. at the election of the Receiving Party, return all Confidential Information to the Disclosing Party or destroy such Confidential Information as soon as reasonably practicable; and
     3. to the extent reasonably practicable, expunge any Confidential Information stored in any computer, word processor or other electronic device or medium; and
     4. if the Disclosing Party requests in writing, provide to the Disclosing Party a written statement executed by an officer of the Receiving Party confirming compliance with this Clause 5.1 by the Receiving Party and Permitted Recipients to whom Confidential Information is disclosed.
  2. Clause 5.1 shall not require the Receiving Party or any of its Permitted Recipients to return, destroy or expunge Confidential Information that is:
     1. required to be retained for compliance with any Applicable Law or order by which it is bound or to meet the requirements of professional indemnity insurance arrangements;
     2. retained to comply with any *bona fide* internal records management or document retention policy or any backup electronic media made pursuant to archival processes in the ordinary course of business; or
     3. stored on computer, word processor or other electronic device or medium which destruction or expungement is not reasonably practicable, provided that such retained Confidential Information is not generally accessible beyond the need for disaster recovery or similar operations.
  3. Notwithstanding the expiry of the Term, any Confidential Information retained in accordance with Clause 5.2 shall continue to be kept and held by the Receiving Party and Permitted Recipients in accordance with the terms of this Agreement for so long as it is retained, and such Confidential Information shall not be used for any purpose other than the purposes for which Confidential Information are retained.
