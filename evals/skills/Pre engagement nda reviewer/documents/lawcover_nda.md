**Whereas:**

1. Lawcover is an APRA-authorised general insurer under the *Insurance Act 1973* (Cth), providing insurance and related services to legal professionals operating in NSW and across Australia.
2. The Recipient is a consultant engaged by the Department of Communities & Justice (**DCJ**) to provide a report on the solicitors’ Professional Indemnity Insurance (**PII**) market in NSW (**Report**). The Report will be used to inform the DCJ’s advice to the Attorney General regarding ABC Insurance Pty Ltd’s request that the Attorney General approve its PII policy under s. 95 of the *Legal Profession Uniform Law Application Act*.
3. To assist with the preparation of its Report, the Recipient has asked for information to better understand the current insurance arrangements in NSW. Lawcover is currently the only insurer of an approved policy for solicitors in NSW. Lawcover’s data will assist the Recipient form an accurate view of the state of the legal PII market in NSW.
4. For this purpose, Lawcover intends to disclose certain confidential information to the Recipient, which the Recipient agrees to protect in accordance with the terms of this NDA.

**The Parties agree as follows:**

# The purpose of this NDA is to set out the terms on which Confidential Information will be disclosed to and protected by the Recipient, in order to assist the Recipient provide a Report to the DCJ.

# Term of this NDA

# This NDA will take effect on and from the date on which the last Party signs this NDA and continue for a period of three months, or until superseded by another agreement between the Parties, whichever occurs sooner.

# Notwithstanding clause 2.1, clauses 3 – 10 will survive termination of this NDA.

Confidential Information excludes information that:

* + 1. is publicly available at the time of its disclosure under this NDA; or

    3. following disclosure under this NDA, becomes available to the Recipient from a source which is not bound by any obligation of confidentiality to Lawcover in relation to such information; or
    4. is independently developed by the Recipient without the aid of Confidential Information.
