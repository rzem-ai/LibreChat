# Operative Provisions

# Interpretation

Definitions

## Unless a contrary intention appears, these meanings apply in this document:

**AEMO Data** means data of AEMO, data provided to AEMO by participants in an AEMO Market (including Registered Participants, metering providers, metering data providers and metering coordinators as those terms are defined in the National Electricity Rules), protected information (as defined in the National Electricity Law), and any other data provided to AEMO by third parties.

**AEMO Information** means any information about AEMO, any AEMO Market (including participants in an AEMO Market) and information about AEMO’s Systems and sites.

**AEMO Market** means any market operated or administered by AEMO (including any related power system or transmission system) and any information service provided by AEMO under arrangements contemplated by or under the Energy Legislation.

**Commencement Date** means the date specified in the Details.

**Communication** means a notice, approval, consent, agreement, request or other communication required by or given under this Agreement.

**Confidential Information** means any information:

* + 1. associated with AEMO’s business or affairs (including AEMO Data and AEMO Information), regardless of the form of Disclosure or the medium used to store it; and
    2. that is provided or prepared by, made available to, learnt or received by or otherwise becomes known to the Recipient, in relation to the Permitted Purpose,

and includes any copies of that information or material derived from that information.

**Disclose** means to provide, permit, allow, cause or enable access to Confidential Information, and **Disclosing** and **Disclosure** have corresponding meanings.

**Energy Legislation** means:

* + 1. the National Electricity Law; National Electricity Regulations and National Electricity Rules;
    2. the National Gas Law; National Gas Regulations and National Gas Rules;
    3. the National Energy Retail Law, National Energy Retail Regulations and National Energy Retail Rules;
    4. any legislation applying any of the foregoing in a jurisdiction;
    5. the Electricity Industry (Wholesale Electricity Market) Regulations 2004 (WA) and Wholesale Electricity Market Rules;
    6. the Gas Services Information Regulations 2012 (WA); and
    7. any other statute or legislative instrument of the Commonwealth or a State or Territory providing for the establishment, operation or administration of a market for energy or an energy system; and
    8. any instrument or procedure made under any of the foregoing.

**Intellectual Property Rights** means all intellectual property rights, including all current and future registered rights in respect of patents, copyright, designs, moral rights, trademarks, trade secrets, knowhow, confidential information, inventions and discoveries and all other intellectual property as defined in Article 2 of the *Convention Establishing the World Intellectual Property Organisation of July 1967*.

**Law** means any Commonwealth, state, or local legislation, judicial, administrative, or regulatory decrees, judgments, awards or orders and all common laws and equity.

**Personal Information** means any information or an opinion about an identified individual, or an individual who is reasonably identifiable (whether the information or opinion is true or not and whether the information or opinion is recorded in a material form or not), which forms part of the Confidential Information.

**Representative** means any director, officer, employee, contractor, agent, professional advisor, auditor or and other authorised representative of the Recipient who needs to have access to Confidential Information for the Permitted Purpose.

**Systems** means software, hardware (including peripherals and storage media), equipment, networks, other technology and communications links and data stored on or processed by the foregoing and includes information about its technical design, configuration, access methods (including APIs) and security.

**Term** means the period specified in the Details.
