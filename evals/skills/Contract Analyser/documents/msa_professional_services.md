# Master Services Agreement — Professional Services

**Client:** Solstice Financial Holdings
**Supplier:** Vantage Advisory Group Pty Ltd
**Effective Date:** 15 January 2026

## Clause 6 — Delay in Performance

6.1 Where a Statement of Work specifies a Delivery Date, the Supplier must give the Client written notice of any anticipated delay as soon as practicable, and in any event no later than 2 Business Days before the Delivery Date.

6.2 The notice must set out the reason for the delay, the impact on downstream deliverables, and a proposed revised Delivery Date.

6.3 The Client's Engagement Lead must respond to a delay notice within 3 Business Days, either accepting the revised date or escalating to the Steering Committee.

6.4 Failure by the Supplier to notify a delay in accordance with Clause 6.1 entitles the Client to withhold payment of the affected Milestone Fee until the deliverable is accepted.

## Clause 7 — Variations

7.1 A Variation to a Statement of Work must be documented using the Variation Request template in Appendix C.

7.2 Only the Client's Engagement Lead and the Supplier's Engagement Partner have authority to approve a Variation.

7.3 A Variation Request must be submitted at least 10 Business Days before the proposed effective date and must specify the impact on fees, timetable, and resourcing.

7.4 Verbal instructions to vary the Services are not binding. Work performed on the basis of an unapproved Variation is undertaken at the Supplier's own risk and is not chargeable.

## Clause 8 — Fees and Invoicing

8.1 The Supplier shall submit invoices on a monthly basis, no later than the 5th Business Day of the following month.

8.2 Each invoice must reference the relevant Statement of Work number, Milestone achieved, and be accompanied by a signed Milestone Acceptance Certificate.

8.3 Invoices must be denominated in AUD and submitted by email to accountspayable@solsticefinancial.example.

8.4 The Client shall pay undisputed invoices within 45 days of the invoice date.

8.5 Amounts genuinely disputed in good faith may be withheld pending resolution, provided the undisputed portion is paid on time.

## Clause 9 — Subcontracting

9.1 The Supplier must not subcontract or delegate any part of the Services to a third party without the Client's prior written consent.

9.2 Consent requests must be accompanied by the proposed subcontractor's conflict check results and confidentiality undertaking.

9.3 The Client may refuse consent at its absolute discretion where the Supplier proposes to subcontract core advisory work.

9.4 Any approved subcontractor must comply with all confidentiality and data-handling obligations under this Agreement.

## Clause 10 — Key Milestones

10.1 The Supplier shall deliver the Interim Findings Report within 20 Business Days of the Effective Date.

10.2 The Supplier shall deliver the Draft Final Report within 45 Business Days of the Effective Date, for the Client's review and comment.

10.3 The Supplier shall present the Final Report to the Steering Committee within 55 Business Days of the Effective Date.
