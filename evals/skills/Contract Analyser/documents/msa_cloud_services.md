# Master Services Agreement — Cloud Platform Services

**Agreement Number:** MSA-CPL-2023-112  
**Customer:** Harbour City Health Network ("Customer")  
**Supplier:** CloudPeak Solutions Pty Ltd ("Supplier")  
**Commencement Date:** 15 July 2023  
**Initial Term:** 3 years  

---

## 1. Definitions and Interpretation

### 1.1 Definitions

"Authorised Users" means personnel of the Customer who are authorised to access the Platform.  
"Business Day" means a day that is not a Saturday, Sunday, or public holiday in New South Wales.  
"Platform" means the CloudPeak managed cloud platform described in Schedule 1.  
"Service Levels" means the performance metrics set out in Schedule 3.  
"Variation" has the meaning given in Clause 5.  

---

## 2. Services

### 2.1 Service Description

The Supplier shall provide managed cloud platform services including infrastructure hosting, platform monitoring, incident management, and quarterly capacity reviews as detailed in Schedule 1.

### 2.2 Service Levels

(a) The Supplier shall maintain Platform availability of 99.95% measured monthly, excluding scheduled maintenance windows.  
(b) Critical incidents (Priority 1) must be acknowledged within 15 minutes and resolved within 4 hours.  
(c) The Supplier shall provide a monthly Service Level Report within 5 Business Days of each month end.  
(d) If the Supplier fails to meet Service Levels for 3 consecutive months, the Customer may terminate under Clause 9.2.

### 2.3 Quarterly Reviews

The Supplier shall conduct quarterly service reviews with the Customer's IT Director. A written review report must be circulated at least 5 Business Days prior to the review meeting.

---

## 3. Fees and Payment

### 3.1 Monthly Service Fees

(a) The Customer shall pay a monthly service fee of AUD $185,000 (exclusive of GST) as set out in Schedule 2.  
(b) Fees shall be reviewed annually on each anniversary of the Commencement Date. Any increase shall not exceed 3% per annum unless agreed in writing.

### 3.2 Invoicing

(a) The Supplier shall issue invoices on the 1st Business Day of each calendar month for services to be provided in that month (invoiced in advance).  
(b) Each invoice must reference: (i) the Agreement Number, (ii) the billing period, (iii) itemised service charges, (iv) any Service Level credits applied, (v) GST amount, and (vi) total payable.  
(c) Invoices shall be submitted via the Customer's procurement portal at https://procurement.harbourcityhealth.internal.  
(d) All amounts are in Australian Dollars (AUD).

### 3.3 Payment Terms

(a) The Customer shall pay valid invoices within 21 calendar days of receipt.  
(b) The Customer may withhold payment of disputed amounts provided it notifies the Supplier in writing within 7 Business Days of invoice receipt, detailing the disputed items.  
(c) Late payments attract interest at 1.5% per month on the overdue amount, compounding monthly.

---

## 4. Delays and Force Majeure

### 4.1 Delay Notification

(a) If either party anticipates a delay to any scheduled deliverable or service obligation, it must notify the other party in writing within 3 Business Days.  
(b) The notice must specify: (i) the obligation affected, (ii) the anticipated delay period, (iii) root cause analysis, and (iv) proposed corrective actions.

### 4.2 Delay Management

(a) The non-affected party shall respond to the delay notice within 5 Business Days, confirming acceptance of the corrective plan or proposing amendments.  
(b) Where a delay is caused by the Customer (e.g., delayed access provisioning or approval), the Supplier shall be granted a day-for-day extension.  
(c) Where a delay is caused by the Supplier, the Supplier must implement corrective actions within 10 Business Days or be subject to Service Level penalties under Schedule 3.

### 4.3 Force Majeure

Neither party shall be liable for failure to perform obligations due to Force Majeure events, provided the affected party notifies the other within 2 Business Days of the event and uses reasonable endeavours to mitigate its effects. If Force Majeure continues for more than 60 calendar days, either party may terminate by giving 15 Business Days' written notice.

---

## 5. Variations

### 5.1 Variation Process

(a) Either party may propose a Variation to this Agreement by submitting a written Variation Request to the other party's Contract Manager.  
(b) A Variation Request must include: (i) detailed description of the proposed change, (ii) rationale, (iii) impact assessment on fees, service levels, and timelines, and (iv) implementation plan.  
(c) The receiving party must acknowledge the Variation Request within 3 Business Days.  
(d) The parties shall negotiate the Variation in good faith and respond substantively within 15 Business Days of acknowledgement.  
(e) No Variation is binding unless executed in writing by both parties' authorised representatives.  
(f) Verbal agreements or email exchanges do not constitute valid Variations.

### 5.2 Emergency Variations

For urgent security or compliance-related changes, the Customer may direct the Supplier to implement changes immediately. The Supplier must comply within 24 hours and a formal Variation Request must be submitted retrospectively within 5 Business Days.

---

## 6. Subcontracting

### 6.1 General Prohibition

The Supplier shall not subcontract, delegate, or assign any part of the Services without the prior written approval of the Customer's Chief Information Officer.

### 6.2 Approval Process

(a) A subcontracting request must be submitted at least 30 Business Days before the proposed start date.  
(b) The request must include: (i) identity and corporate details of the proposed subcontractor, (ii) scope of work, (iii) security credentials and certifications, (iv) data handling practices, and (v) proposed contractual controls.  
(c) The Customer shall respond within 15 Business Days. Failure to respond is deemed a refusal.  
(d) Approved subcontractors must execute a tripartite deed binding them to the confidentiality, security, and data handling obligations of this Agreement.

### 6.3 Supplier Liability

The Supplier remains fully liable for the acts and omissions of any subcontractor. A breach by a subcontractor is deemed a breach by the Supplier.

### 6.4 Subcontractor Removal

The Customer may require removal of any subcontractor by giving 15 Business Days' written notice. The Supplier must effect the removal and transition services within 30 Business Days without disruption to the Customer.

---

## 7. Confidentiality

### 7.1 Obligations

Each party shall maintain the confidentiality of the other's Confidential Information and shall not disclose it to any third party without prior written consent, except as required by law.

### 7.2 Data Sovereignty

All Customer data must be stored and processed within Australia. The Supplier must certify compliance within 5 Business Days of the Commencement Date and annually thereafter.

---

## 8. Reporting and Communication

### 8.1 Monthly Reports

The Supplier shall provide a comprehensive monthly operations report within 10 Business Days of each month end, covering: incidents, availability metrics, change activity, and capacity utilisation.

### 8.2 Incident Notification

Critical incidents must be reported to the Customer's IT Operations Manager within 30 minutes of detection. A root cause analysis report must be provided within 5 Business Days of incident resolution.

---

## 9. Term and Termination

### 9.1 Termination for Convenience

Either party may terminate this Agreement by giving 90 calendar days' written notice.

### 9.2 Termination for Persistent Breach

The Customer may terminate this Agreement by giving 30 Business Days' notice if the Supplier fails to meet Service Levels for 3 or more consecutive months.

### 9.3 Termination for Material Breach

Either party may terminate immediately upon written notice if the other party commits a material breach and fails to remedy it within 30 Business Days of receiving notice.

---

## 10. Transition Assistance

### 10.1 Obligation

Upon termination or expiry, the Supplier shall provide transition assistance for a period of up to 6 months at the rates specified in Schedule 4. The Supplier must commence transition planning within 10 Business Days of receiving a termination notice.
