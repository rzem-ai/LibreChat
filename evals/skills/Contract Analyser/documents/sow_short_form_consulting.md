# Statement of Work — Brand Strategy Consulting Engagement

**Client:** Aurora Consumer Brands
**Supplier:** Halcyon Consulting Collective
**Start Date:** 3 February 2026

This is a short-form engagement letter. It is not divided into numbered clauses; instead it uses the section headings below.

## Timing and Delays

If either of us can't hit a deadline in the project plan (Appendix A), we'll let the other side know as soon as we reasonably can, ideally within a few days, and talk about a new date. There's no formal penalty structure in this engagement — we're relying on both sides acting in good faith to keep things on track. Persistent delays of more than two weeks without explanation may be treated as a material breach.

## Changing the Scope

If new work comes up that isn't in the original brief, the Supplier will flag it to the Client's marketing director before starting, along with a rough estimate of extra time and cost. Nothing should be treated as in-scope unless the Client has confirmed it in writing (email is fine).

## Fees and Billing

The Supplier will send an invoice at the end of each month for hours worked that month, based on the rate card in Appendix B. Invoices go to the Client's finance inbox and are payable within 30 days. Amounts are in AUD and do not include GST, which is added separately.

## Deliverables

- Brand audit workshop output — due 2 weeks after kickoff
- Draft positioning framework — due 5 weeks after kickoff
- Final brand guidelines deck — due 8 weeks after kickoff

## Other Notes

This engagement letter does not include any terms about using subcontractors, agency staff, or third parties to help deliver the work — that topic simply isn't addressed anywhere in this document. Confidentiality obligations are covered separately in the signed NDA dated 20 January 2026, which is incorporated by reference.
