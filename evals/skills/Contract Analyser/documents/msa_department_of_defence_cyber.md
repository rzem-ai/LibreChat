# Master Services Agreement — Cyber Resilience Program

**Client:** Department of National Defence, Directorate of Cyber Capability
**Supplier:** Ironclad Systems Integration Pty Ltd
**Classification:** OFFICIAL — Sensitive
**Effective Date:** 1 April 2026

## Clause 1 — Delay Notification

1.1 Where the Supplier anticipates a delay to a Deliverable affecting classified network infrastructure, it must notify the Directorate's Contract Authority within 1 Business Day.

1.2 Delays affecting Defence-classified systems require immediate escalation to the Program Security Officer.

## Clause 2 — Variations

2.1 Variations to this Agreement affecting security-classified work packages must be approved by the Directorate's Contract Authority and countersigned by the Defence Security Vetting Agency.

## Clause 3 — Invoicing

3.1 The Supplier shall invoice monthly through the Department's approved defence procurement portal, referencing the relevant Work Package number and security clearance level of personnel involved.

## Clause 4 — Subcontracting and Security Clearances

4.1 No subcontractor may be engaged on this Agreement without a current Negative Vetting Level 1 security clearance issued by the Australian Government Security Vetting Agency.

4.2 All subcontracting arrangements require approval from the Department of National Defence Security Division.

## Clause 5 — Deliverables

5.1 The Supplier shall deliver the classified Network Hardening Report within 30 Business Days of the Effective Date.

5.2 The Supplier shall maintain personnel with appropriate security clearances for the duration of the Cyber Resilience Program.

This Agreement and all associated work packages relate to Defence cyber capability uplift and are subject to Defence security classification handling requirements.
