# Statement of Work — IT Infrastructure Modernisation

**Document Reference:** SOW-2024-0847  
**Client:** Meridian Financial Services Pty Ltd ("Client")  
**Provider:** Apex Consulting Group ("Provider")  
**Effective Date:** 1 March 2024  
**Expiry Date:** 28 February 2025  

---

## 1. Scope of Services

### 1.1 Overview

The Provider shall deliver IT infrastructure modernisation services to the Client, including migration of on-premises workloads to Azure cloud, implementation of a zero-trust security framework, and decommissioning of legacy data centres.

### 1.2 Deliverables

(a) Cloud migration strategy document — due within 30 Business Days of the Effective Date.  
(b) Azure landing zone deployment — due within 60 Business Days of the Effective Date.  
(c) Application migration (Tranche 1: 40 applications) — due within 120 Business Days of the Effective Date.  
(d) Security framework implementation — due within 150 Business Days of the Effective Date.  
(e) Legacy data centre decommissioning plan — due within 180 Business Days of the Effective Date.  

---

## 2. Governance and Reporting

### 2.1 Steering Committee

The Provider shall attend monthly Steering Committee meetings and provide a written status report no later than 3 Business Days prior to each meeting.

### 2.2 Weekly Status Reports

The Provider shall submit a weekly status report to the Client's Project Manager by 5:00 PM AEST each Friday, covering progress, risks, and issues.

### 2.3 Change Requests

(a) Either party may request a change to this SOW by submitting a written Change Request to the other party's Project Manager.  
(b) A Change Request must include: (i) description of the proposed change, (ii) impact on timeline, (iii) impact on Fees, and (iv) any risks introduced.  
(c) The receiving party must acknowledge receipt within 2 Business Days.  
(d) Both parties must assess and respond to the Change Request within 10 Business Days of acknowledgement.  
(e) No change shall be implemented until both parties have signed a written Change Order.  
(f) If the parties cannot agree on terms within 20 Business Days of the initial Change Request, either party may escalate the matter under Clause 7.

---

## 3. Fees and Invoicing

### 3.1 Fixed Fees

The total fixed fee for the Services is AUD $2,450,000 (exclusive of GST), payable in milestones as set out in Schedule B.

### 3.2 Invoice Requirements

(a) The Provider shall issue invoices monthly in arrears, by the 5th Business Day of each calendar month.  
(b) Each invoice must include: (i) SOW reference number, (ii) milestone reference, (iii) description of services rendered, (iv) hours worked per resource category, (v) applicable GST, and (vi) Provider's bank account details.  
(c) Invoices shall be submitted electronically to accounts@meridianfs.com.au.  
(d) All invoices must be denominated in Australian Dollars (AUD).

### 3.3 Payment Terms

(a) The Client shall pay undisputed invoices within 30 calendar days of receipt.  
(b) If the Client disputes an invoice, it must notify the Provider in writing within 14 calendar days of receipt, specifying the disputed amount and reasons.  
(c) Undisputed portions shall be paid in accordance with Clause 3.3(a).  
(d) Late payments shall accrue interest at the rate of 2% per annum above the Reserve Bank of Australia cash rate.

---

## 4. Delays

### 4.1 Notification

(a) If either party becomes aware that a deliverable or milestone will be delayed, it must notify the other party in writing within 5 Business Days of becoming aware of the delay.  
(b) The notification must include: (i) the affected deliverable, (ii) the expected duration of the delay, (iii) the cause of the delay, and (iv) proposed mitigation actions.

### 4.2 Delay Assessment

(a) Upon receipt of a delay notification, the non-delaying party shall have 10 Business Days to assess the impact and respond in writing.  
(b) If the delay is attributable to the Client (including failure to provide access, information, or approvals), the Provider shall be entitled to an extension of time equal to the period of the Client-caused delay.  
(c) If the delay is attributable to the Provider, the Provider must submit a remediation plan within 15 Business Days.

### 4.3 Prolonged Delays

If a delay exceeds 40 Business Days and no remediation plan has been agreed, the non-delaying party may terminate this SOW by giving 20 Business Days' written notice.

---

## 5. Subcontracting

### 5.1 Approval Required

(a) The Provider shall not subcontract any part of the Services without the prior written consent of the Client.  
(b) A request for consent must be submitted at least 20 Business Days prior to the proposed subcontractor's commencement date and must include: (i) the subcontractor's name and ABN, (ii) the scope of work to be subcontracted, (iii) the subcontractor's relevant qualifications, and (iv) evidence of appropriate insurance coverage.

### 5.2 Conditions of Subcontracting

(a) The Provider remains fully responsible for the performance of any subcontractor as if the work were performed by the Provider itself.  
(b) All subcontractors must execute a deed of confidentiality in the form approved by the Client.  
(c) The Client may revoke consent to a subcontractor at any time by giving 10 Business Days' written notice if, in the Client's reasonable opinion, the subcontractor's performance is unsatisfactory.

---

## 6. Confidentiality and Security

### 6.1 Security Clearances

All Provider personnel accessing Client systems must hold a valid Baseline security clearance or equivalent. The Provider must notify the Client within 2 Business Days if any personnel's clearance is revoked or suspended.

### 6.2 Data Handling

All Client data processed under this SOW must remain within Australian-based data centres at all times. The Provider must certify compliance with this requirement within 10 Business Days of the Effective Date.

---

## 7. Dispute Resolution

### 7.1 Escalation

(a) Disputes shall first be escalated to the respective Project Managers, who shall attempt resolution within 5 Business Days.  
(b) If unresolved, the dispute shall be escalated to the respective Executive Sponsors within 10 Business Days.  
(c) If still unresolved after 20 Business Days from initial escalation, either party may refer the matter to mediation.

---

## 8. Term and Termination

### 8.1 Termination for Convenience

Either party may terminate this SOW by giving 60 calendar days' written notice to the other party.

### 8.2 Termination for Cause

Either party may terminate this SOW immediately upon written notice if the other party commits a material breach and fails to remedy the breach within 20 Business Days of receiving written notice of the breach.
