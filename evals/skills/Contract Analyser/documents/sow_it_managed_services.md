# Statement of Work — IT Managed Services

**Client:** Northbridge Retail Group
**Supplier:** Meridian Technology Partners Pty Ltd
**Effective Date:** 1 March 2026

## 1. Term and Delay

1.1 The Supplier shall deliver the Managed Services in accordance with the timetable set out in Schedule 2.

1.1(a) If the Supplier becomes aware that it will not meet a Milestone Date, it must notify the Client in writing within 3 Business Days of becoming aware of the likely delay, specifying the cause and the revised estimated date.

1.1(b) Upon receipt of a delay notice, the Client shall, within 5 Business Days, either (i) approve a revised Milestone Date, or (ii) request a recovery plan from the Supplier.

1.1(c) Where a delay exceeds 15 Business Days and is not excused under Clause 1.2 (Force Majeure), the Client may apply Service Credits calculated in accordance with Schedule 4.

1.2 Force Majeure delays must be notified within 2 Business Days and do not attract Service Credits provided the Supplier uses reasonable endeavours to mitigate impact.

## 2. Change Process

2.1 Either party may propose a change to the Services by submitting a Change Request Form to the Change Control Board.

2.1(a) The initiating party must submit the Change Request at least 10 Business Days before the desired implementation date.

2.1(b) The Change Control Board shall assess and respond within 5 Business Days of receipt, approving, rejecting, or requesting further information.

2.1(c) No change to scope, price, or timetable is binding unless recorded in a signed Change Order. Work performed without an approved Change Order is not billable.

## 3. Invoicing

3.1 The Supplier shall invoice the Client monthly in arrears, on the last Business Day of each calendar month.

3.1(a) Each invoice must include: a description of Services performed, the applicable Milestone or period, hours worked (where time and materials apply), the PO number, and applicable GST.

3.1(b) Invoices shall be submitted in AUD via the Client's Coupa portal.

3.1(c) Payment is due within 30 days of the Client's receipt of a valid invoice.

3.1(d) Late payments accrue interest at 2% above the RBA cash rate per annum, calculated daily.

## 4. Subcontracting

4.1 The Supplier may not subcontract any material part of the Services without the Client's prior written consent, such consent not to be unreasonably withheld.

4.1(a) Any request to subcontract must be submitted at least 15 Business Days prior to the proposed subcontractor commencing work, together with evidence of the subcontractor's insurance and security clearances.

4.1(b) The Supplier remains fully liable for the acts and omissions of any approved subcontractor as if they were its own.

## 5. Deliverables and Service Levels

5.1 The Supplier shall provide a Monthly Service Report within 5 Business Days of month end.

5.2 The Supplier shall maintain a Service Desk availability of 99.5% during Business Hours, measured monthly.

5.3 Security patching of critical vulnerabilities must be completed within 7 calendar days of a patch being made available by the vendor.
