# Statement of Work — Data Analytics Platform Implementation

**SOW Reference:** DA-SOW-2024-003  
**Client:** Pinnacle Retail Group Ltd ("Client")  
**Consultant:** DataForge Advisory Pty Ltd ("Consultant")  
**Start Date:** 1 June 2024  
**End Date:** 30 November 2024  

---

## 1. Services

### 1.1 Scope

The Consultant shall design, build, and deploy a customer analytics platform on the Client's existing Snowflake environment, including data ingestion pipelines, a customer segmentation model, and executive dashboards.

### 1.2 Deliverables and Milestones

| ID | Deliverable | Due Date |
|---|---|---|
| D1 | Data architecture design document | 28 June 2024 |
| D2 | Ingestion pipeline (MVP) | 31 July 2024 |
| D3 | Customer segmentation model v1 | 31 August 2024 |
| D4 | Executive dashboard suite | 30 September 2024 |
| D5 | User acceptance testing sign-off | 31 October 2024 |
| D6 | Production deployment and handover | 30 November 2024 |

### 1.3 Acceptance Criteria

(a) Each deliverable shall be subject to the Client's acceptance testing within 10 Business Days of submission.  
(b) If the Client identifies defects, it must provide a written defect notice within 5 Business Days of the acceptance testing period.  
(c) The Consultant shall remedy identified defects within 10 Business Days of receiving the defect notice.

---

## 2. Fees and Invoicing

### 2.1 Fee Structure

The total fee is AUD $780,000 (exclusive of GST), structured as:
- 20% upon execution of this SOW ($156,000)
- 80% payable upon milestone completion as per Schedule A

### 2.2 Invoicing

(a) The Consultant shall submit invoices within 5 Business Days of each milestone acceptance by the Client.  
(b) Invoices must contain: (i) SOW reference number, (ii) milestone ID and description, (iii) amount due, (iv) GST, (v) cumulative spend to date, and (vi) bank transfer details.  
(c) Invoices shall be emailed to ap@pinnacleretail.com.au in PDF format.  
(d) Currency: Australian Dollars (AUD).

### 2.3 Payment

(a) Payment shall be made within 14 Business Days of receipt of a valid invoice.  
(b) Disputed invoices: the Client must notify the Consultant within 7 Business Days of receipt, specifying the grounds for dispute.  
(c) Late payment penalty: 1% per month on overdue amounts, calculated from the due date.

---

## 3. Change Management

### 3.1 Change Requests

(a) Changes to scope, timeline, or fees must be requested via a formal Change Request submitted to the other party's Engagement Manager.  
(b) The Change Request must detail: (i) proposed change, (ii) justification, (iii) effort estimate, (iv) fee impact, and (v) timeline impact.  
(c) The receiving party shall acknowledge within 3 Business Days and provide a substantive response within 7 Business Days.  
(d) Changes are only effective when documented in a signed Change Order.

### 3.2 Minor Adjustments

Adjustments that do not impact fees or extend the timeline by more than 5 Business Days may be agreed via email between the respective Engagement Managers, followed by a confirmatory Change Order within 10 Business Days.

---

## 4. Delays

### 4.1 Delay Notification

(a) Either party must notify the other within 2 Business Days of becoming aware of any circumstance likely to cause a delay to a milestone.  
(b) The notification must identify: (i) the milestone affected, (ii) estimated delay duration, (iii) root cause, and (iv) proposed remediation steps.

### 4.2 Client-Caused Delays

If a delay is attributable to the Client (including late provision of data, delayed feedback, or unavailability of key personnel), the Consultant shall receive:  
(a) An extension of time equal to the Client-caused delay; and  
(b) Reimbursement of any additional costs incurred at the day rates specified in Schedule B, subject to prior written approval.

### 4.3 Consultant-Caused Delays

(a) If a delay is attributable to the Consultant, the Consultant must provide a remediation plan within 5 Business Days.  
(b) If the cumulative delay exceeds 20 Business Days and no agreed remediation plan is in effect, the Client may terminate this SOW under Clause 6.2.

---

## 5. Subcontracting

### 5.1 Permitted Subcontractors

The Client consents to the Consultant engaging the following pre-approved subcontractors:
- Quantix Data Engineering (data pipeline development)
- Vizual Analytics Co (dashboard design)

### 5.2 Additional Subcontractors

(a) Engagement of any subcontractor not listed in Clause 5.1 requires the Client's prior written consent.  
(b) Consent requests must be submitted at least 15 Business Days before the proposed start date, including: (i) subcontractor details, (ii) scope, (iii) qualifications, and (iv) insurance certificates.  
(c) The Client shall respond within 10 Business Days. Silence does not constitute consent.

### 5.3 Consultant Responsibility

The Consultant is responsible for all subcontractor performance and must ensure subcontractors comply with the confidentiality and data protection obligations of this SOW.

---

## 6. Termination

### 6.1 Termination for Convenience

Either party may terminate this SOW by giving 30 calendar days' written notice. The Client shall pay for all accepted milestones and pro-rata work completed to the termination date.

### 6.2 Termination for Cause

The Client may terminate immediately upon written notice if:  
(a) The Consultant commits a material breach not remedied within 15 Business Days of notice; or  
(b) Cumulative delays exceed 20 Business Days without an agreed remediation plan.

---

## 7. Intellectual Property

### 7.1 Ownership

All deliverables and work product created under this SOW shall be the property of the Client upon payment. The Consultant retains ownership of its pre-existing IP and tools, granting the Client a perpetual, non-exclusive licence.

---

## 8. Communication

### 8.1 Progress Reporting

The Consultant shall provide fortnightly progress reports to the Client's Engagement Manager by close of business each second Friday, covering: work completed, upcoming activities, risks, and blockers.

### 8.2 Escalation

Unresolved issues must be escalated from Engagement Manager to Executive Sponsor within 5 Business Days. If unresolved at Executive Sponsor level within 10 Business Days, either party may invoke Clause 6.
