# Healthcare AI Implementation – Newsletter/Viva Engage Input

**Project Name:** Clinical Decision Support System Implementation

**Client:** Leading Australian Healthcare Group

**Team Lead:** Dr. Michael Robertson

**Team Members:** Dr. Michael Robertson, Sarah Chen (Clinical Lead), Marcus Thompson (Technical Lead), Priya Gupta (Change Management), Robert Williams (Data Scientist)

**Project Duration:** January 2025 – May 2025

## Executive Summary

We successfully deployed an AI-powered clinical decision support system across a 450-bed healthcare group, improving diagnostic accuracy and reducing clinician time on administrative tasks. The system processes patient data in real-time to flag potential drug interactions, suggest diagnostic pathways, and provide evidence-based treatment recommendations.

## Key Achievements

- Deployed to 12 clinical departments across 3 hospital locations
- Achieved 94% clinician adoption rate within 90 days (exceeding 75% target)
- Reduced average diagnostic review time by 28%
- Prevented 23 critical medication interactions in the first 60 days
- Training delivered to over 800 clinical staff with 96% satisfaction rating

## Impact Metrics

- Patient safety improved with zero adverse events attributed to system issues
- Clinician feedback was overwhelmingly positive, with requests for expanded functionality
- ROI achieved within 6 months through efficiency gains
- System is now being expanded to 2 additional healthcare networks
