## Business Context

The client is a major Australian bank facing increasing customer expectations for seamless digital payment experiences. Legacy payment processing systems are creating bottlenecks and limiting the bank's ability to respond to market competitors offering faster, more intuitive solutions. The bank needed a comprehensive modernisation strategy that would replace its aging payment infrastructure while maintaining regulatory compliance and ensuring zero downtime during the transition.

## Delivery Scope

We delivered a 6-month engagement that encompassed:
- Current state assessment of legacy payment systems (2 weeks)
- Target state architecture design aligned with industry standards (4 weeks)
- Detailed implementation roadmap with 3-phase rollout (2 weeks)
- Selection and integration of a modern payment gateway platform (8 weeks)
- Testing and cutover planning (4 weeks)

The delivery team comprised 12 full-time resources, including architects, engineers, and compliance specialists. We worked closely with the client's internal teams across technology, operations, risk, and compliance.

## Business Impact

- Reduced payment processing time by 60% (from 48 hours to 18 hours for international transfers)
- Improved customer satisfaction scores by 35% in post-launch surveys
- Achieved 99.99% system uptime during migration
- Created a foundation for future API-driven innovation
- Estimated cost savings of AUD $2.8M annually through operational efficiency
