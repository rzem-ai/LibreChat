# Retail Supply Chain Optimisation – Multi-Content Input

**Project Name:** Omnichannel Supply Chain Redesign

**Client:** National Retail Group

**Team Lead:** Jennifer Walsh

**Project Timeline:** September 2024 – April 2025

## Context

A national retail group with 280 stores across Australia wanted to improve inventory management and reduce out-of-stock incidents that were costing them 12% in lost sales. Their existing supply chain was fragmented, with separate inventory systems for online and physical stores unable to share real-time visibility.

## Solution Delivered

- Unified inventory management platform connecting all 280 stores and distribution centres
- Real-time demand forecasting using AI and historical sales patterns
- Automated replenishment algorithms reducing manual ordering by 70%
- Integration with major supplier EDI systems for automated purchase orders
- Mobile app enabling store staff to check inventory and initiate orders

## Results Achieved

- Out-of-stock incidents reduced by 58%
- Inventory carrying costs down 22% through better demand forecasting
- Ordering time per store reduced from 4 hours per week to 30 minutes
- Sales uplift of 8.5% in first 90 days (attributed partly to improved availability)
- Supply chain visibility improved from 40% to 98% across the network

## Team Contributions

Jennifer Walsh led the engagement working closely with the client's Chief Supply Chain Officer. The delivery team included supply chain specialists, data engineers, and retail domain experts who understood the nuances of omnichannel retail operations.

## Notable Success Factors

- Strong collaboration between IT and merchandising teams
- Phased rollout starting with highest-volume stores
- Real-time dashboards providing immediate visibility into supply chain health
- Supplier partnerships strengthened through EDI integration
