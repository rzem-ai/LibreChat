# Public Sector Modernisation – Case Study Input

**Project Name:** Government Services Integration Platform

**Client:** Australian Federal Government Department

**Engagement Duration:** March 2025 – August 2025

## Problem Context

The client department operates multiple disparate citizen-facing services (welfare, licensing, permits, registrations) across separate legacy systems. Citizens face significant friction navigating between systems and providing duplicate information. The department lacked a unified view of citizen journeys, making it difficult to deliver proactive, integrated services.

## Solution Approach

We implemented a unified services integration platform that:
- Consolidated three separate citizen portals into a single, intuitive interface
- Created a shared identity and data layer enabling seamless authentication
- Built real-time status tracking for applications across all services
- Implemented proactive notification system to guide citizens through processes
- Deployed using cloud-native architecture with modern microservices

The engagement involved 25 FTE across discovery, architecture, development, and change management. We worked across five operational divisions within the department.

## Key Outcomes

- Reduced average citizen task completion time by 45%
- Achieved 65% increase in online transaction volume (vs. in-person visits)
- Improved first-contact resolution rate from 52% to 78%
- Enabled richer analytics for policy decision-making
- Foundation for future service innovations

## Major Win Factors

- Strong executive sponsorship from the Deputy Secretary
- Effective change management resulting in high staff adoption
- Phased rollout approach minimised risk and build momentum
- Open, collaborative partnership between Deloitte and departmental teams
- Cloud architecture positioned department for future scalability

## Additional Comments

This was a transformational engagement that demonstrates the power of integrated thinking across service design, technology, and change management in the public sector. The platform is now being used as a case study within the government sector for digital service integration best practices.
