# Master Services Agreement — Professional Advisory Services

**Agreement ID:** MSA-PAS-2024-0056  
**Principal:** Southland Infrastructure Corporation ("Principal")  
**Advisor:** Brampton Partners Pty Ltd ("Advisor")  
**Effective Date:** 1 January 2024  
**Term:** 5 years (with 2-year extension option)  

---

## 1. Engagement Structure

### 1.1 Framework

This Agreement establishes a framework under which the Principal may engage the Advisor to provide professional advisory services through individual Statements of Work. Each SOW will specify scope, fees, deliverables, and timelines.

### 1.2 Minimum Commitment

The Principal commits to a minimum annual spend of AUD $1,200,000 (exclusive of GST) across all SOWs issued under this Agreement. If the minimum is not met, a shortfall fee of 15% of the deficit shall be payable within 60 calendar days of each anniversary.

---

## 2. Fees and Invoicing

### 2.1 Rate Card

Services shall be charged at the rates set out in Schedule 1 (Rate Card). Rates are fixed for the first 2 years and subject to CPI adjustment thereafter, capped at 4% per annum.

### 2.2 Invoicing Requirements

(a) The Advisor shall invoice the Principal monthly in arrears, within 10 Business Days of each month end.  
(b) Each invoice must include:  
  (i) Agreement ID and relevant SOW reference(s);  
  (ii) Billing period;  
  (iii) Breakdown by resource name, role, hours worked, and applicable rate;  
  (iv) Expense claims with receipts (if applicable);  
  (v) GST;  
  (vi) Total amount due;  
  (vii) Advisor's banking details for electronic funds transfer.  
(c) Invoices exceeding AUD $100,000 must be accompanied by a statutory declaration confirming the accuracy of hours claimed.  
(d) Submission method: electronic submission via the Principal's Vendor Portal (vendorportal.southlandinfra.com.au).  
(e) Currency: Australian Dollars (AUD).

### 2.3 Payment Terms

(a) The Principal shall pay undisputed invoices within 28 calendar days of receipt.  
(b) Disputed amounts must be notified in writing within 10 Business Days of receipt, with specific identification of disputed line items.  
(c) The Advisor may suspend services if undisputed amounts remain unpaid for more than 45 calendar days, provided it gives 10 Business Days' written notice of intended suspension.  
(d) Interest on late payments: 2.5% per annum above the RBA cash rate, calculated daily and compounding monthly.

---

## 3. Change Management

### 3.1 SOW Variations

(a) Variations to an existing SOW must be proposed in writing by either party's Engagement Lead.  
(b) The proposal must include: (i) description of proposed change, (ii) reason for the change, (iii) impact on deliverables and timeline, (iv) revised fee estimate, and (v) resource implications.  
(c) The receiving party shall acknowledge within 2 Business Days.  
(d) Both parties must negotiate and respond within 10 Business Days of acknowledgement.  
(e) No variation is effective until documented in a signed Variation Order referencing the original SOW.  
(f) If parties cannot agree within 20 Business Days, the matter shall be referred to the Relationship Governance Board under Clause 8.

### 3.2 New SOW Issuance

The Principal may issue a new SOW at any time. The Advisor must confirm acceptance or propose amendments within 10 Business Days of receipt.

---

## 4. Delay Management

### 4.1 Early Warning

(a) Each party has a continuous obligation to provide early warning of any matter that may cause delay to SOW deliverables.  
(b) Formal notification must be provided within 3 Business Days of the party becoming aware of the potential delay.  
(c) The notification must include: (i) SOW reference, (ii) deliverable affected, (iii) nature and estimated duration of delay, (iv) root cause, and (v) proposed mitigation strategy.

### 4.2 Response and Assessment

(a) The non-delaying party must acknowledge the notification within 2 Business Days.  
(b) Both parties shall meet (in person or virtually) within 5 Business Days to agree on a revised schedule or mitigation approach.  
(c) Agreed schedule changes must be documented via a Variation Order within 10 Business Days of the meeting.

### 4.3 Consequences of Delay

(a) Advisor-caused delays: If cumulative delay on any SOW exceeds 15 Business Days without agreed mitigation, the Principal may:  
  (i) Apply liquidated damages of AUD $5,000 per Business Day of delay (capped at 10% of the affected SOW value); or  
  (ii) Terminate the affected SOW under Clause 7.2.  
(b) Principal-caused delays: The Advisor shall receive a day-for-day extension plus reimbursement of documented standby costs at 50% of the applicable day rate.

---

## 5. Subcontracting

### 5.1 General Rule

The Advisor shall perform the Services using its own personnel unless subcontracting is expressly approved under this Clause.

### 5.2 Approval Process

(a) The Advisor must submit a subcontracting request to the Principal's Procurement Director at least 25 Business Days before the proposed engagement date.  
(b) The request must include:  
  (i) Full corporate details of the proposed subcontractor (including ABN/ACN);  
  (ii) Key personnel CVs;  
  (iii) Scope of work to be subcontracted;  
  (iv) Rationale for subcontracting;  
  (v) Security clearance status of subcontractor personnel;  
  (vi) Evidence of professional indemnity insurance (minimum AUD $10 million);  
  (vii) Proposed fee arrangement between Advisor and subcontractor.  
(c) The Principal shall approve or refuse within 15 Business Days. Refusal must include written reasons.  
(d) Approval may be subject to conditions including: security vetting, direct reporting obligations, or cap on subcontractor hours.

### 5.3 Subcontractor Management

(a) The Advisor must maintain a register of all approved subcontractors, updated quarterly and provided to the Principal on request.  
(b) Subcontractors must comply with all obligations of this Agreement as if they were the Advisor's employees.  
(c) The Advisor must conduct annual performance reviews of each subcontractor and provide a summary to the Principal within 20 Business Days of each review.

### 5.4 Removal and Replacement

(a) The Principal may require removal of any subcontractor by giving 10 Business Days' written notice.  
(b) The Advisor must effect the removal and propose a replacement within 20 Business Days without service disruption.  
(c) If no acceptable replacement is proposed within the 20 Business Day period, the Principal may source the replacement directly at the Advisor's cost.

---

## 6. Confidentiality and Data Protection

### 6.1 Confidentiality Period

Confidentiality obligations survive for 7 years after termination of this Agreement.

### 6.2 Personnel Obligations

All Advisor personnel (including subcontractors) must sign individual confidentiality undertakings within 5 Business Days of commencing work under any SOW.

### 6.3 Data Localisation

All Principal data must remain within Australian borders. The Advisor must provide a data sovereignty compliance certificate within 10 Business Days of the Effective Date and annually thereafter by 31 January each year.

---

## 7. Termination

### 7.1 Termination for Convenience

Either party may terminate this Agreement by giving 120 calendar days' written notice. Active SOWs will continue to their completion unless separately terminated.

### 7.2 Termination of Individual SOW

The Principal may terminate an individual SOW by giving 20 Business Days' written notice if:  
(a) Cumulative Advisor-caused delay exceeds 15 Business Days; or  
(b) The Advisor commits a material breach of the SOW not remedied within 15 Business Days of notice.

### 7.3 Immediate Termination

Either party may terminate immediately if:  
(a) The other party becomes insolvent or enters administration; or  
(b) A breach of Clause 6 (Confidentiality) occurs.

---

## 8. Governance

### 8.1 Relationship Governance Board

A Relationship Governance Board comprising senior representatives of both parties shall meet quarterly to review performance, resolve disputes, and approve strategic direction. Meeting agendas must be circulated at least 7 Business Days in advance.

### 8.2 Dispute Resolution

(a) Disputes shall first be raised at Engagement Lead level, with 5 Business Days to resolve.  
(b) If unresolved, escalation to the Relationship Governance Board within 10 Business Days.  
(c) If the Board cannot resolve within 20 Business Days, either party may refer to independent mediation.  
(d) Pending resolution, both parties must continue to perform their obligations under this Agreement.

---

## 9. Reporting

### 9.1 Monthly Activity Reports

The Advisor shall provide monthly activity reports within 7 Business Days of each month end, covering: hours consumed vs budget, deliverable progress, risk register updates, and upcoming activities.

### 9.2 Annual Reconciliation

Within 30 Business Days of each anniversary of the Effective Date, the Advisor shall provide an annual reconciliation report comparing actual spend against the minimum commitment under Clause 1.2.
