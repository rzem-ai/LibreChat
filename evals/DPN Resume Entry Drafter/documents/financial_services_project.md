# Project Details — Payments Modernisation

## Client and Project
- **Client:** Meridian National Bank
- **Project:** Core Payments Modernisation Program
- **Role:** Senior Business Analyst
- **Duration:** March 2024 to November 2024
- **Industry:** Financial Services

## Project Description

Meridian National Bank undertook a major transformation of its legacy payments infrastructure to comply with the New Payments Platform (NPP) real-time settlement requirements. The project involved migrating from a batch-based SWIFT messaging system to a real-time ISO 20022 message format, impacting retail, corporate, and institutional banking channels.

I was embedded within the payments squad as a Senior Business Analyst, working closely with the product owner, solution architects, and an offshore development team in India. My role focused on eliciting requirements from business stakeholders, translating regulatory mandates into user stories, and facilitating cross-team alignment between the compliance, technology, and operations teams.

## Key Achievements

- I led the requirements gathering for 14 payment message types across three channels, producing over 120 user stories accepted into the backlog within two sprints
- I designed the end-to-end payment flow diagrams using Visio and facilitated 8 design workshops with stakeholders from compliance, operations, and technology
- I identified a gap in the exception handling process that would have caused failed transactions to be lost — this led to a new reconciliation sub-process that saved the bank an estimated $2.4M in potential regulatory fines
- I created the UAT test strategy and coordinated testing across three environments, achieving 97% first-pass test success rate
