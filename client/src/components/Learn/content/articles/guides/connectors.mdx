## What it is

A connector is a secure link between MyAssist and an approved source, such as a research database or a business system. It lets MyAssist use information that a file can’t hold. MCP stands for Model Context Protocol, the standard these links use.

## Why it matters

Chat works from the files you provide and the context already in your conversation. When the answer depends on trusted information held elsewhere, a connector lets MyAssist draw on it.

## Where to find it

Select Connectors to see the list available to you. You can’t add connectors yourself today.

## When to use one

Use a connector when:

- You need market, industry or company research
- Information sits in an approved business system
- You need trusted organisational knowledge beyond uploaded files
- You want MyAssist to draw on one or more approved sources

You may not need one when:

- The information is already in the files you’ve provided
- You only need help drafting, summarising or brainstorming
- The chat already has enough context to complete the task

## Connector examples

Connectors you might see include DESC, IBISWorld, SharePoint, GIMS, AFR News, KX and DPN. Which connectors you see depends on your access.

Take preparing for a client meeting:

- Without a connector, you ask “Summarise this client proposal.” MyAssist uses the proposal and the context you’ve provided.
- With a connector, you ask “Summarise this client proposal and identify relevant industry trends using IBISWorld.” MyAssist combines the proposal with that approved source.
