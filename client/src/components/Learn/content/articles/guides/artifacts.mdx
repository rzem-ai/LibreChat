## What it is

An artifact is something MyAssist builds that you can use, such as a microsite, diagram or interactive tool. It opens in a separate panel alongside your chat. You generate artifacts from a chat, a skill or an agent, so they aren’t a separate capability.

## Why it matters

Artifacts let you refine and share a deliverable instead of copying text out of a response. Ask for a change in the chat and the artifact updates.

## Where to find it

Artifacts are turned on by default. Ask MyAssist to create one, and it appears in the panel beside your chat.

## What you can build

Ask for the thing you want to use:

- An interactive workshop agenda
- A webpage or microsite
- A process diagram
- A calculator or planning tool
