The Agentic Ecosystem is the shared foundation that brings together the technology, data, governance, skills, connectors and tools needed to build, deploy and manage enterprise AI agents securely and at scale.

## A connected foundation for enterprise AI

It provides the capabilities that power AI experiences across Deloitte, including MyAssist NextGen.

By connecting models, data, skills, agents and systems, the Agentic Ecosystem helps reduce the need to move between disconnected tools. This makes it easier to streamline workflows and deliver greater impact.

## How people access it through MyAssist NextGen

MyAssist NextGen is the front door to the Agentic Ecosystem, where people can discover, use and build approved AI agents and skills.

Through the Agentic Ecosystem, agents can access the information and systems they need, work together across workflows and help complete complex tasks.

**Think of it like a connected team**

Instead of coordinating every step yourself, you set the objective, guide the work and review the outcome. Behind the scenes, agents work together to help get the task done.
