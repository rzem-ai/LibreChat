## What the Agentic Ecosystem is

The Agentic Ecosystem is the shared foundation behind enterprise AI agents. It brings together the technology, data, governance, skills, connectors and tools needed to build, deploy and manage them securely and at scale. It powers AI experiences across Deloitte, including MyAssist.

The opportunity isn’t in any single AI tool. It’s in connecting models, data, skills and agents so they work together securely and help you deliver greater impact.

For the full story, read [the announcement on DAIS News](https://ausdeloitte.sharepoint.com/sites/DAISNews/SitePages/Introducing-Deloittes-Agentic-Ecosystem.aspx).

## The four priorities

Four priorities guide how Deloitte builds and scales AI in FY27:

- Speed: move faster by reusing what already works instead of building everything from scratch
- Data: connect agents securely to the information and systems they need through shared, governed sources
- Flexibility: keep the architecture open so the right models and technologies can be used as they evolve
- Agents: enable teams to design, build and deploy agents while managing cost

## Where MyAssist fits

MyAssist is the front door to the Agentic Ecosystem. It’s where you discover, use and build approved AI agents and skills.

<Callout type="analogy">

Think of it as a connected team. You set the objective, guide the work and review the outcome. Agents work together behind the scenes, so you don’t coordinate every step yourself.

</Callout>

MyAssist will be the main way most people reach the Agentic Ecosystem, but not the only way. Other approved tools can draw on the same skills, connectors and services. MyAssist and Microsoft Copilot remain Deloitte’s primary enterprise AI platforms.

## What happens next

The MyAssist team is testing and refining the experience ahead of a broader firmwide rollout later this year. Expect examples of agents in action, practical guidance and learning opportunities over the coming months.
