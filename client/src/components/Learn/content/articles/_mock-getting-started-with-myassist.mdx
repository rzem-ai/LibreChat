Three habits make every chat after this one better.

## Start a new chat for each topic

Keeping topics separate gives the model cleaner context and makes your history easier to search.

## Use projects to group related work

Projects keep files, instructions and chats for one piece of work in a single place.

## Keep the context panel open

The right-hand context panel shows what MyAssist can see, so you know what it is working from.
