title: Use Connectors
updated: 16 October 2026
---

Use Connectors when MyAssist NextGen needs approved information from a source or business system beyond the files you have provided or the context already in your conversation.

## When approved information or systems are needed

Start with Chat. Add a Connector when the answer depends on information held in an approved research source, knowledge repository or business system.

**Use a Connector when:**

- You need market, industry or company research
- Information is held in an approved business system
- You need trusted organisational knowledge beyond uploaded files
- You want MyAssist to draw on one or more approved sources

**You may not need one when:**

- The information is already in the files you have provided
- You only need help drafting, summarising or brainstorming
- Chat already has enough context to complete the task

## What is a Connector?

A Connector gives MyAssist access to an approved information source or business system. It acts as a bridge, allowing MyAssist to use relevant information from that source in its response.

**Example connectors**

DESC, IBISWorld, SharePoint, GIMS, AFR News, KX and DPN. Connector availability and access may vary.

## Example: preparing for a client meeting

**Without a Connector**

“Summarise this client proposal.”

MyAssist uses the proposal and the context already provided.

**With a Connector**

“Summarise this client proposal and identify relevant industry trends using IBISWorld.”

MyAssist can combine the proposal with information from the selected approved source.
