## Why MyAssist exists

AI at work can feel fragmented. Different tools, models and sources of information don’t always connect, so you move between them and join the pieces yourself.

MyAssist is the front door to the Agentic Ecosystem, the shared foundation that connects technology, data and governance across Deloitte. The team rebuilt MyAssist as an agent-first experience. Read about the platform behind it in [What is the Agentic Ecosystem](/learn/articles/agentic-ecosystem).

## What MyAssist helps you do

Start with the task: the business problem or piece of work in front of you. Describe what you need and what a good result looks like, then explore how MyAssist helps. Add technology only where it earns its place.

You set the objective, guide the work and review the outcome. MyAssist helps you think through a problem, draft and summarise, and work from your own documents. It can also draw on approved sources and repeat a proven process.

## Tailor your environment

An environment is everything an agent can see and use while it works, such as your files, approved connectors and skills. MyAssist works with what its environment gives it. Shaping the environment steers the results.

Because you choose what goes in, you control what MyAssist works from. Add your team’s skills and approved sources, and MyAssist applies your way of working instead of a generic one.
